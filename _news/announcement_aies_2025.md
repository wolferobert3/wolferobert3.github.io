Excited to share that two new papers probing social norms surrounding human interactions with AI chatbots have been accepted to [**AIES 2025**](https://www.aies-conference.com/2025/). The first is a study drawing on the principles of [Nonviolent Communication to inform AI-mediated interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11149), and the second is a study of [privacy norms among U.S. users of LLM-based chatbots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06760). Congrats to the amazing group of students whose work in the User Empowerment directed research group made the second study possible, and especially to the talented first author [Sarah Tran](https://ischool.uw.edu/people/phd/profile/saraht45)!
