Glad to have been a part of two creative new NLP papers probing the limits of LLMs! The first is a new paper from [Bin Han](https://beanham.github.io/menu/about.html) [studying the capabilities of LLMs to perform spatial integration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05009) tasks on urban data, and will appear at [**COLM 2025**](https://colmweb.org/). The second was led by [Dexter Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pRO6b2QAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Bingbing Wen](https://bbwen.github.io/) and presented at [**ACL Findings 2025**](https://2025.aclweb.org/); this [study develops new approaches to confidence estimation in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00582), and examines whether LLMs mirror human patterns of overconfidence.
